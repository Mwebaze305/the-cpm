JOIN THE TEAM

Researchers wanted

We're looking for colleagues and students to research with us. You work in your own community, with guidance from our experts.

APPLY → WHO CAN APPLY

What researchers do

  1. I

    Gather evidence

    Study one of our four focus areas where you live, using documents, data and people's experience.

  2. II

    Work with an expert

    One of our experts advises you on your question, your sources and your methods.

  3. III

    Publish under your name

    You write short, sourced briefings that end with what should be done. Once approved, they appear under your name.

Who can apply

  • Students at university: undergraduate, master's or PhD.
  • Researchers at any stage of their career, in universities, research institutes, government or civil society.
  • From or based in the Great Lakes (Uganda, DR Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, Kenya, Tanzania), the Nile Basin (Ethiopia, Sudan, Egypt) or Southeast Asia (an ASEAN member state).
  • Interested in at least one of our four focus areas.
  • You can write in English, French, Thai or Kiswahili.

What to expect

  • This is a volunteer role. You work at your own pace and agree each piece of work with us.
  • Free to join. No fees at any stage.
  • Your work carries your name, and your views are your own: you take part in a personal capacity.
  • If you work for a government, we will not ask you to write on contested political disputes.
