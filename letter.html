Why a letter of credence

Chiang Rai, Thailand · 30 October 2026

Excellency,

When an ambassador arrives in a new capital, the first act is to present a letter of credence to the head of state. The letter does not argue a case. It asks for trust: the bearer speaks in good faith and deserves to be heard.
