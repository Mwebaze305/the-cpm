Ethics

Everyone is welcome to write with us. Here is all you need to know.

  • Anyone can send us an idea, a draft or a proposal. You do not need permission to start.
  • We read every piece before it is published and work on it with you.
  • Ethics comes in where it is needed. If your work involves people, for example interviews, we think it through together at that stage.
  • Mistakes are corrected openly.
  • We are independent. We do not speak for any government or institution.
