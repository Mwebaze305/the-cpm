Ethics

Simple principles for how The CPM writes and publishes. They guide finished, published work. They never stop anyone from starting.

Where they apply

  • The weekly letters.
  • Notes published through Exchange of Notes.
  • Early work needs no approval from us. Ideas, questions, drafts and research proposals, up to your first and second chapters, are yours to develop freely.

In the letters

  • Accurate. Facts come from named sources, listed in APA style.
  • Corrected. When a letter gets something wrong, we fix it and say so.
  • Independent. The letters do not speak for any government or institution.
  • Fair. People and countries are described as they are, including views we disagree with.
  • Credited. Images are our own or free to use, with a credit line.

In Exchange of Notes

  • Your own words. Cite the work you build on.
  • Shared credit. Both authors are named equally.
  • Care for people. If you speak with people for your research, tell them how their words will be used, and keep vulnerable people, such as refugees, unnamed unless they agree.
  • Your university's rules stay with your university. If your institution requires ethics approval for fieldwork, that is between you and it. We never ask for approval before you start.

Corrections and concerns

If you spot an error or have a concern, reply to the letter's email. We will look into it and respond.

These principles will grow with the publication.
