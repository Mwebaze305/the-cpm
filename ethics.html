INDEPENDENCE AND STANDARDS

The rules we hold ourselves to.

A think tank is useful only if people can trust its work. That is what credence means. These are our rules.

We name problems honestly, and we never stop there: every piece we publish ends with a practical solution.

Independence

  • The Credence Letters takes no institutional positions. Each letter, briefing and note carries the conclusions of its authors, and no one else's.
  • We owe no allegiance to any government, party or institution, and we do not speak for any.
  • Team members write in a personal capacity. Their views are their own, not those of their employers or of any government.

Funding

  • The Credence Letters receives no outside funding. It runs on the volunteer time of its team.
  • If that changes, we will publish here every funder, the amount and what it pays for.
  • We will not accept money from a source we cannot identify, or money that comes with conditions on what we find or publish. Funders will have no say over our conclusions.

Evidence and review

  • Every piece names its sources, and every figure is checked against its source.
  • Every letter and briefing ends with a short "What should be done" section.
  • The editor reviews every piece before it is published.
  • Mistakes are corrected openly.
  • Where work involves people, for example interviews, we think the ethics through before it starts.

Citing our work

  • Every letter and briefing ends with an APA reference you can copy.
  • You are welcome to quote our work, with credit and a link.

Write with us

  • Anyone can send us an idea, a draft or a proposal. You do not need permission to start.
  • We read every piece before it is published and work on it with you.
