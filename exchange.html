OUR LEAD PROGRAMME · SDG 17

Exchange of Notes

A research partnership between the Great Lakes, the Nile Basin and Southeast Asia, three Global South regions working toward sustainable development. Scholars and practitioners from all three regions share ideas, research shared policy questions together, and turn what they find into better policy for their communities.

PRESENT YOUR NOTE → HOW IT WORKS

Three regions, shared problems

The Great Lakes, the Nile Basin and Southeast Asia face many of the same problems: drug-resistant malaria and long displacement at their borders, conflicts that cross borders and test relations between governments, upstream dams on shared rivers and the daily work of cross-border trade. Their scholars and practitioners rarely work on them together.

Exchange of Notes connects them directly, so ideas travel between Kampala, Kinshasa, Dar es Salaam, Addis Ababa, Cairo, Bangkok, Jakarta and Hanoi without passing through anyone else first. The aim is better policy, and real improvement for the communities we come from.

17

Built on SDG 17

Sustainable Development Goal 17 asks countries to strengthen South–South cooperation and knowledge sharing (target 17.6) and to build partnerships with civil society (target 17.17). Exchange of Notes applies both to research: partnerships between scholars and practitioners of the Global South, for sustainable development in their own communities.

How it works

  1. I

    Present your note

    Tell us who you are, where you work or study, and the policy question you care about.

  2. II

    Be paired

    We pair you with a scholar or practitioner in another region who is working on a related question.

  3. III

    Exchange and publish

    Together you write a short policy note. Selected notes are published on The Credence Letters, with both authors credited, in the languages of both communities.

Who can take part

  • Scholars: students from final-year undergraduate to PhD level, and researchers at any stage.
  • Practitioners: people working in government, civil society, health, development and community organisations.
  • Based in a Great Lakes country (Uganda, DR Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, Kenya, Tanzania), a Nile Basin country (Ethiopia, Sudan, Egypt) or an ASEAN member state.
  • You can write in English, French, Thai or Kiswahili.

Our commitments

  • Free. No fees at any stage.
  • Both authors are credited equally on every published note.
  • Independent. We do not speak for any government or institution.
  • Your details are used only to pair you and to contact you about the programme.
