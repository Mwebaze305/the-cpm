Exchange of Notes

A student research partnership between the Great Lakes and Southeast Asia. Students on both sides share ideas, research a shared policy question together, and carry what they find back to their own communities.

Two regions, shared problems

The Great Lakes and Southeast Asia face many of the same policy questions: shared rivers, displaced people, cross-border trade and competition between larger powers. Their universities rarely work on them together.

Exchange of Notes connects students directly, so ideas travel between Kampala, Kinshasa, Kigali, Bangkok, Jakarta and Hanoi without passing through anyone else first. The aim is research that local policymakers can use.

17

Built on SDG 17

Sustainable Development Goal 17 asks countries to strengthen South–South cooperation and knowledge sharing (target 17.6) and to build partnerships with civil society (target 17.17). Exchange of Notes applies both to research: partnerships between students of the Global South, for sustainable development in their own communities.

How it works

  1. I

    Present your note

    Tell us who you are, where you study and the policy question you care about.

  2. II

    Be paired

    We pair you with a student in the other region who is working on a related question.

  3. III

    Exchange and publish

    Together you write a short policy note. Selected notes are published on The CPM, with both authors credited, in the languages of both communities.

Research themes

Pick one of these, or bring your own question.

  • Refugees and host communities
  • Shared rivers: the Nile and the Mekong
  • Cross-border trade and migration
  • Governance and accountability
  • Peace and security
  • Diplomacy between Africa and Southeast Asia

Who can take part

  • Final-year undergraduates, master's and PhD students, and early-career researchers.
  • At a university in a Great Lakes country (Uganda, DR Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, Kenya, Tanzania) or in an ASEAN member state.
  • You can write in English, French, Thai or Kiswahili.

Our commitments

  • Free. No fees at any stage.
  • Both authors are credited equally on every published note.
  • Independent. We do not speak for any government or institution.
  • Your details are used only to pair you and to contact you about the programme.
